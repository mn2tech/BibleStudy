Lesson Five

Living Strong

Strength & Endurance

Strength is a vital characteristic for men who desire to live pure and purposeful lives.

The apostle Paul knew this better than anyone. He lived a life of constant persecution and resistance from others; he endured through storms, shipwrecks, snake bites, and starvation. He had to overcome his past; resist the temptations of single-life; fight the effects of cultural swings — if anyone knew what was needed to fulfill their purpose it was Paul.

2 Corinthians 4:8–9 (NIV)

We are hard pressed on every side, but not crushed; perplexed, but not in despair; persecuted, but not abandoned; struck down, but not destroyed.

Philippians 4:13 (NIV)

I can do all this through him who gives me strength.

1 Samuel 30:6 (NKJV)

Now David was greatly distressed, for the people spoke of stoning him, because the soul of all the people was grieved… But David strengthened himself in the Lord his God.

The Christian life is not for the weak; it is not built for comfort, it's built for endurance (endure = to harden into something that lasts).
James 1:3–4 (NIV)

For you know that when your faith is tested, your endurance has a chance to grow. So let it grow, for when your endurance is fully developed, you will be perfect and complete, needing nothing.

We will never endure as men if we do not get strong. Living strong is not optional, it's required.

1 Corinthians 16:13 (NIV)

Be on your guard; stand firm in the faith; be courageous; be strong.

Ephesians 6:10 (NLT)

A final word: Be strong in the Lord and in his mighty power.

Is Jesus Lord of your life?
