The Man in the Arena

The Battle

We are in a battle and every man needs to know what victory feels like. The apostle Paul reminds us of the arena that we are in and invites us onto the battlefield:

1 Timothy 6:12 (NIV)

Fight the good fight of the faith.

2 Timothy 2:3–4 (NLT)

Endure suffering along with me, as a good soldier of Christ Jesus. Soldiers don't get tied up in the affairs of civilian life, for then they cannot please the officer who enlisted them.

Jude 1:3 (NLV)

Now I must write to you and tell you to fight hard for the faith...

Your life has foes.

For those who have put their trust in Jesus, you are promised a new life.

2 Corinthians 5:17 (NIV)

If anyone is in Christ, the new creation has come: The old has gone, the new is here!

Romans 8:37 (NIV)

Learn how to .

The arena is real. The battles we face are real; they are challenging; and if we don't learn to fight against them, they will wreak havoc on every part of our life.

John 10:10 (NIV)

The thief comes only to steal and kill and destroy...

The enemy brings opposition that, if not dealt with, becomes:

› — it's not easily resolved; it creates difficulty and complications.
› — it's present everywhere—you can't easily avoid it.
› — it keeps coming back, refusing to stop; it doesn't go away.
› — it has the power to shape and influence your life—it has real impact.
You are already in the arena. Don't be a punching bag for the devil. Don't be naive to traps and temptations. Wise up, ready yourself, and fight back!

Some of us have given in to defeat. This happens because:

  • You haven't seen changes that you desired.
  • The battles and temptations you face feel too difficult and overwhelming.
  • You think the odds are stacked against you.
  • Problems stack on top of problems.
  • You've been told your whole life. You have taken on the label of defeated.
  • You've seen generational battles last generations.
  • You've subscribed to a win some, lose some mentality or way of living.
  • You feel that God can forgive others, but he wouldn't forgive you.

Key Takeaways
Lesson Two Summary

Your new life has old foes.

Our old nature continues to battle our new nature.

Our minds need to be continually renewed.

We are entangled by old patterns and perspectives.

The enemy knows our weak spots.

Learn how to fight.

Know your opponents. Engage in the battle. Let God define what's possible. Take action and stick with it.
