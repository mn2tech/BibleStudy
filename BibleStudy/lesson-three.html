Where Temptation Lives

Sexual temptation involves your eyes, your mind, and your heart; however, sexual sin is a sin against the body.

The Eyes
Job 31:1 (AMP)

I have made a covenant (agreement) with my eyes; How then could I gaze [lustfully] at a virgin?

Matthew 6:22–23 (NLT)

Your eye is like a lamp that provides light for your body. When your eye is healthy, your whole body is filled with light. But when your eye is unhealthy, your whole body is filled with darkness.

The Mind
Romans 8:6 (AMP)

For those who are living according to the flesh set their minds on the things of the flesh [which gratify the body], but those who are living according to the Spirit, [set their minds on] the things of the Spirit [His will and purpose].

Mark 7:21–23 (CEV)

Reflection Questions
Question 1
Why do you think sexual temptation is hitting men so hard right now? Where have you seen its impact — personally or around you?
Question 2
Read the different versions of Ephesians 5:3. Put into your own words the Bible's standard regarding sexual immorality.
Question 3
What does 1 John 3:8, Colossians 2:15, and 1 Corinthians 15:56–57 tell us about the degree of victory one can have over sin? What would be different in your life if you lived in this reality?
Question 4
Sexual temptation engages the eyes, mind, and heart. Which is your weakest area right now?
Question 5
Why is exposing sin important? What are the implications of unexposed sin?
Question 6
What are you currently engaging in that is fueling temptation (apps, shows, social media, habits, etc.)? How do you need to handle these so that you are cutting off the temptation vs. managing it?
Question 7
Why is healing from sexual sin important? How does one take steps toward healing from sexual sin?
